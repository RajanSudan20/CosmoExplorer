# AI Assistance & Transparency Record

During the development of **CosmoExplorer** for the NASA Space Apps Challenge, AI tools were used transparently for the following tasks:

1. **Code Structure & Boilerplate:** Assisting with Python Streamlit layout syntax and component arrangement.
2. **Debugging:** Quick error identification and fixing syntax issues in the code.
3. **Content Drafting:** Structuring space trivia, metrics formats, and quiz options.

*Note: All project integration, final testing, concept design, and team coordination were managed independently by the team developer.*
